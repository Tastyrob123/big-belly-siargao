The website, eight tools and thirty-two pieces for print and Instagram, in the order of a day at the shack on Tourism Road, from 11:30 AM to 9:30 PM.

From 11:30 AMThe stickers

11:30 AM · At the counter

Order, pay, review

Three inserts for the acrylic stand, each with front and back: the free iced tea for an honest Google review in two versions, and «Pay as you order» with «Choose your protein». Below them a chalkboard for the counter, three napkins and coasters for slush, shake and iced tea.

1 PM · On the tray

Trays, wraps and bags

Paper for the steel trays, wraps for the burgers with a label to tick the patty, two fries boxes, sleeves for the slush cup, paper bags and a card for every delivery bag. Below: a flag for every burger, a box for delivery, the receipt and two stamps.

4 PM · On Tourism Road

For hostels and surf camps

Flyers and a poster with the map of General Luna, Tourism Road and Big Belly on it, an A-board and a long banner for the road.

The flyers

7 PM · Under the neon

Seventy-six stickers

Seventy-six stickers on seven sheets: the neon sign, the house’s own lines, the nine burgers, what hangs in the shack, the drinks, the sides and dots for the patty, each sheet as a print file with cut line and as SVG. To take home: a tote bag and three pins.

9:30 PM · On Instagram

The next thirty posts

The next thirty posts in a phone you can scroll: burgers, fried chicken and loaded fries, the shack by day and by night, the entrance on Tourism Road, the counter and the marlin, with nine posts set in the colours of the house. Below the phone six stories in 1080 × 1920.

Website, tools and the proposal

Four pages for the phone and the computer: Home, Menu, The Shack and Visit. Eight tools in the look of the house: Control panel, Edit website, Menus & price lists, Inquiries & chat, Visitors, Brand book, Sticker editor, Print & feed. A film of the website, and the proposal as a PDF.
